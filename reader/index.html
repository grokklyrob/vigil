Vigil

A cloister. Hold still.

DESCENT — κένωσις

The emptying is not a metaphor. Heat leaves the hands. The jaw unclenches because there is nothing left to bite. What remains is a diameter.

ENFLESHING — σάρξ

Flesh is the only instrument that can stop. The spine is a column. Weight goes into the heels. Blood is loud if you let it be.

HIDDENNESS

A cloister is a wall that keeps the world from rehearsing you. Sit where the light does not reach the face. Do not announce the sitting.

OPENING — σπορά

The seed is not an idea. It is a small hard thing put into dark. After that the work is weather.

CLOSING

Put the machine down. Stand. Walk until you can name one object you did not bring with you. Then stay with it until it is only itself.
